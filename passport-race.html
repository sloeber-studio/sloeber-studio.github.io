How to play

  1. Claim (1 pt): take turns, zoom into a part of the world, tap a free country and answer right to stamp it.
  2. Border links 🔗 (+1 pt each): own two countries that share a land border and a route line links them.
  3. Lock 🔒 (+1 pt): tap one of your countries and beat a hard question to lock it. You can lock up to 3.
  4. 👑 HQ (+2 pts): tap one of your locked countries and beat the HQ challenge. Your HQ can never be stolen, and it shields 🛡️ your countries next to it. One HQ each.
  5. Steal 🎯: every 3 right answers earns a ticket (hold up to 2). Spend 1 to steal a normal country, or 2 to mega-steal a locked or shielded one. A country that was just stolen can’t be stolen straight back.
  6. Continent bonus: at the end, leading a continent earns +2 points.
