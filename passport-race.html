How to play

  1. Take turns. Tap a part of the world, then a country to fly there.
  2. Answer right and the country’s stamp goes in your passport.
  3. Every 3 right answers earns a 🎯 steal ticket (you can hold 2). Spend one to try to steal the other player’s country with a hard question.
  4. Tap your own country to try to lock it with a hard question. Locked countries can never be stolen. You can lock up to 3.
  5. A country that was just stolen can’t be stolen straight back on the next turn.
  6. At the end, whoever has the most countries in a continent gets 2 bonus points for it.
