📖 Passport Race Rules

📌 Claiming Territory (+1 pt)

Tap any unclaimed country and answer the geography question to claim it for your passport!

🔗 Border Route Links (+1 pt each)

When you own two countries that share a land border, an animated flight route connects them for bonus points!

🔒 Locking Territories (Max 3)

Tap your claimed country to lock it 🔒. Locked countries require opponents to spend 2 Steal tickets to attack.

👑 Crown Headquarters (+2 pts & Shield 🛡️)

Promote a Locked country into a Crown HQ 👑. HQs are permanently unstealable AND provide a protective Shield 🛡️ to all your adjacent countries!

🎯 Stealing & Mega-Stealing

Earn 1 Steal ticket for every 3 correct answers (max 2 held). Spend 1 ticket to steal an opponent's unlocked country, or spend 2 tickets to Mega-Steal a locked 🔒 country!

🌍 Continent Leadership (+2 pts)

Control the most countries in any continent to earn a +2 Point Continent Bonus!
